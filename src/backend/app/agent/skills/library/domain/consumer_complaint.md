# Consumer Complaint Email

## Overview
A good complaint email gets results. The key is to be specific, factual, and clear about what you want (refund, replacement, apology). Emotional complaints are easily ignored; structured ones are escalated.

## When to Use
- Faulty product purchased from an online retailer (Shopee, Lazada, Tiki, Amazon)
- Wrong order or missing items
- Poor service at a restaurant, hotel, or service provider
- Billing error or unexpected charge
- Unsatisfactory customer service experience

**Do NOT use** for business-to-business complaints (use client_comms) or when the user needs to cancel a subscription (use inbox_cleanup for mass-unsubscribe).

## Template

```
Subject: Complaint: [Order/Reference #] — [Brief issue]

Dear [Company] Support Team,

I'm writing to report an issue with my recent [order/purchase/experience].

Order Details:
• Order #: [number]
• Date: [date]
• Item/Service: [description]
• Issue: [one-line summary — e.g. "Item arrived damaged" / "Wrong size delivered"]

What happened:
[2–3 sentences describing the issue factually. Include photos or screenshots as evidence if available.]

What I'd like:
• [Option A: Refund of [amount] VND]
• [Option B: Replacement shipped by [date]]
• [Option C: Other resolution]

Please let me know how you'd like to proceed. I've attached [photos / screenshots] for reference.

I expect a response within [2–3] business days.

Regards,
[Your Full Name]
[Phone number]
[Email]
```

## Structure (if not using the template)

1. **Subject** — `Complaint: [Order/Ref #] — [Issue in 5 words]`
2. **Reference details** — order number, date, what was ordered
3. **Factual description** — what happened, without emotional language
4. **Desired outcome** — refund, replacement, or other resolution
5. **Deadline** — when you expect a response
6. **Evidence** — mention attached photos/screenshots

## Tone Guide

| Situation | Tone | Example phrasing |
|-----------|------|-----------------|
| Wrong item / missing item | Firm but polite | "I received the wrong item. Please arrange a replacement or refund." |
| Damaged product | Factual + disappointed | "The box arrived crushed and the contents are broken. Photos attached." |
| Billing error | Precise | "I was charged [amount] but the agreed price was [amount]. Please correct this." |
| Poor service | Measured | "My experience on [date] fell short of expectations. Specifically..." |
| Scam / fraud | Urgent | "This charge is unauthorised. Please reverse it immediately." |

## What NOT to do

- **Don't be vague:** "Your product is bad" — specify what exactly is wrong
- **Don't threaten legal action immediately:** Start with the complaint process; escalate if ignored
- **Don't write in all caps:** Emotional = easy to dismiss
- **Don't include multiple issues in one email:** One issue per complaint — open separate tickets
- **Don't forget attachments:** A photo of the damage is worth more than a paragraph

## Follow-up (if no response after 3–5 business days)

```
Subject: Follow-up: [Original subject]

Dear [Company],

I sent a complaint regarding [order #] on [date] but haven't received a response yet.

I'd appreciate an update by [date]. If I don't hear back, I'll escalate through [consumer protection agency / chargeback].

Regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|------|
| Emotional rant | Stick to facts: what, when, what you want |
| No order number | Support can't act without a reference |
| Vague outcome request | State explicitly: refund, replacement, or something else |
| No deadline | Companies prioritise complaints with deadlines |
| One email for multiple issues | Split into separate tickets — they get routed differently |
